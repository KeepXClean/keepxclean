Keep your X timeline and replies clean, readable, and high-signal. 让 X 推荐流与评论区回归清爽、可读、高信噪比。

KeepXClean inspects X’s own timeline and reply responses locally in your browser to strip or collapse high-frequency bots, reply spam, lookalike impersonators, engagement bait, unwanted languages, and promoted ads — without extra X API polling or account sign-in. KeepXClean 在浏览器本地直接解析 X 已返回的信息流与评论数据，按透明规则隐藏或折叠高频刷屏、蓝标水评、高仿冒充、互动引流、非目标语种与推广广告，不额外轮询 X 接口，无需注册登录。

Core Capabilities 核心净化能力

Every interception records explicit rule evidence so you always know why a post was filtered. 每一次拦截均记录当时的触发证据，所有判断依据透明可查、随时可调。

Multi-Dimensional Spam & Bait Rules 多维垃圾与引流规则引擎

Combines lifetime daily posting rate, short-term sliding-window bursts, reply spam & thread-author impersonation detection, giveaway/follow-back bait, hashtag/cashtag stuffing, account quality signals, and promoted ad stripping. 覆盖历史日均发帖、短时滑动窗口连发、蓝标短评/纯图水评、主帖作者高仿冒充识别、抽奖互关引流、标签堆砌、新号默认头像异常关注比以及推广广告剔除。

Custom Regex & Curated Public Keyword Packs 自定义正则与可分项控制的公共词库

Match across tweet text, bio, display name, and expanded URLs with optional reply-only scope and follower exemptions. Built-in public packs can be toggled per rule or copied into your custom list with one click. 支持正文、简介、昵称、链接四类范围匹配及仅评论区生效、粉丝数豁免；内置公共词库支持逐条独立开关，也可一键复制为自定义词条微调门槛。

Tweet Language Allowlist & Blocklist 推文语种白名单 / 黑名单过滤

Keep only the languages you read or mute specific language spam waves, while automatically exempting emoji-only, link-only, and pure media posts (`und` / `zxx` / `qam` / `qct`). 支持「仅看选中语种」或「屏蔽选中语种」两种模式，自动豁免纯图片、纯视频、纯表情与纯链接推文，避免误伤无文本动态。

Quick Block with 4.5s Undo & 3-Stage Status 快捷净空、4.5 秒撤销与三阶段状态跟踪

One-click block saves locally right away and gives you 4.5 seconds to undo before triggering native X block and optional community reporting — reporting local, X, and community outcomes separately without faking external confirmation. 点击净空后立即本地生效并提供 4.5 秒反悔撤销期，随后按偏好执行 X 原生拉黑与可选社区共享；本地、X 原生、社区三阶段状态独立展示，绝不用本地成功冒充外部确认。

Following Exemption & Profile Badges 关注豁免与主页只标不拦

Automatically protects accounts you follow and switches to non-destructive risk badges when you intentionally visit someone’s profile, while still catching spammers quoted or retweeted into your feed. 默认保护已关注账号，主动访问他人主页时仅展示风险提示徽章而不隐藏推文；同时对关注好友引用或转推的陌生黑产账号保持穿透识别。

10 Languages & Clean Light Interface 10 国语言与统一浅色界面

Full native localization across English, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, and Russian across popup, full settings, and in-page X drawer. 全面支持简繁中文、英语、日语、韩语、西语、葡语、法语、德语、俄语 10 种界面语言，覆盖弹窗、完整设置与 X 页内抽屉。
